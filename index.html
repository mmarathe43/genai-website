The problem

Most Indians find out
at the pharmacy, not the doctor.

Lifestyle diseases are silent for years. By the time symptoms show up, the damage is already done. The current system gives people no visibility between annual checkups — if they even do those.

01

Diagnosis comes too late

The average Indian is diagnosed with Type 2 diabetes 4 to 7 years after onset. In that window, organ damage has already begun — kidneys, eyes, nerves.

02

Annual checkups are not enough

A once-a-year lab test gives you one data point. It tells you where you were, not where you are going. Continuous monitoring changes the picture entirely.

03

The system makes it hard to care

Booking a lab, taking half a day off work, collecting paper reports, understanding what they mean. The friction is so high that most people just keep saying next month.

The product

One finger-prick.
Eight minutes.
Everything on your phone.

VitaLoop is a home health monitoring kit that makes it easy for Indian families to track the biomarkers that matter most, every month, without leaving the house.

  • 4-in-1 electrochemical strip reader

    Measures HbA1c, cholesterol, blood pressure and uric acid from a single finger-prick. Results in 8 minutes.

  • Bluetooth health pod + app

    All readings sync automatically and build a rolling 12-month trend line. Family profiles for up to 5 members.

  • Indian calibration model

    Strip accuracy calibrated on Indian blood chemistry — not Western population benchmarks. The only product in India built this way.

  • WhatsApp-native for Tier 2 and 3

    Core nudges, reminders and alerts delivered via WhatsApp. No app download required for basic usage.
